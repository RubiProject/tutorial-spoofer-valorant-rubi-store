## Drivers Necessários

**Baixe o Visual C++ Redistributable Runtimes All-In-One**

[![](https://carbon-7.gitbook.io/carbon/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Ftpucdn.com%2Fapple-touch-icon-v1653048138031.png\&width=300\&dpr=4\&quality=100\&sign=49a1027f\&sv=2)Visual C++ Redistributable Runtimes All-in-One (abril de 2022) DownloadTechPowerUp](https://www.techpowerup.com/download/visual-c-redistributable-runtime-package-all-in-one/)

1. Baixe o Visual C++ Runtimes acima
2. Descompacte o arquivo
3. Execute **install\_all.bat** como administrador
4. Reiniciar o PC

**Baixar DirectX Runtimes**

[https://www.microsoft.com/en-US/download/details.aspx?id=35](https://www.microsoft.com/en-US/download/details.aspx?id=35)

1. Baixe o DirectX acima
2. Desmarque **Instalar a Barra do Bing**

![](https://divine-cheats.gitbook.io/spoofer/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F18024476-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FmnB9nCeIgrnqe5WWCgVo%252Fuploads%252FwEfjbMwhEOqqDjILvftU%252Fimage%2520%2822%29.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D36b43827-9437-4594-8955-051a5ca824cd\&width=768\&dpr=4\&quality=100\&sign=66a29bbd\&sv=2)
